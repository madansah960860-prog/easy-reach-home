Where we deliver

We deliver within the United States only. That means all fifty states, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico. Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico pay the same flat rate as everybody else and reach the free-shipping threshold on exactly the same terms; the only difference is that ground transit takes noticeably longer, and your confirmation email will tell you so.

PO boxes and APO, FPO and DPO military addresses are fine, sent by United States Postal Service. Three items are exceptions on size and need a street address: the Two-Step Wooden Step Stool, the Wide-Handle Laundry Basket and the Woven Storage Basket, Pair. If one of those is ordered to a PO box we will telephone you rather than cancelling the order quietly.

We do not deliver outside the United States and we do not deliver to freight forwarders.

What shipping costs

Standard shipping is $5.50 flat per order, whatever is in it and wherever in the country it is going. It is free once your subtotal reaches $39 before tax. Expedited shipping is a flat $15.50 per order and has no free threshold.

That $5.50 is the lowest flat rate we could make work, and $39 is the lowest free-shipping threshold. There is no handling charge, no packaging charge, no fuel surcharge, no minimum order and no oversize fee on the step stool or the baskets. The figure the cart shows you is the figure you pay, plus sales tax where it applies.

One charge per order
An order that needs two boxes still pays one shipping charge, and you may receive two tracking numbers.

How long it takes

There are two separate periods and it helps to keep them apart.

How long we take to pack your order

- Utensils, peeler, can opener, reaching tool, both clocks, both magnifiers, both bath mats, seat cushion: 1 business day - Kettle, bedside lamp, laundry basket, storage baskets: 1 to 2 business days - Two-Step Wooden Step Stool: 2 business days, because it ships assembled in an oversize carton - Orders placed after 3:00pm Eastern Time, at a weekend, or on a federal holiday start their clock on the next business day

How long the carrier takes

These are the carriers' own estimates for time in transit after we hand a parcel over. They are estimates, not guarantees, and they do not include our packing time.

  • Standard ground, lower forty-eight states: 3 to 6 business days
  • Standard ground, Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico: 6 to 12 business days
  • Expedited, lower forty-eight only: 1 to 3 business days
  • USPS to PO boxes and APO/FPO/DPO: 4 to 10 business days, and a military address can take considerably longer once the parcel enters the military postal system, which no carrier can track

Adding the two together, a standard order to a street address in the lower forty-eight normally arrives four to eight business days after you place it.

Carriers and tracking

We use USPS Ground Advantage, USPS Priority Mail, UPS Ground and UPS 2nd Day Air, choosing on size, weight and destination. We do not offer a carrier choice at checkout because for most of our parcels it would change neither the price nor the day it arrives.

You will receive two emails. The first confirms your order. The second, sent when the parcel leaves us, gives the carrier's name and a tracking number. Tracking usually starts showing movement within a day of that email; a number that has not updated overnight is normal and not a sign of a problem.

If four business days pass with no shipping confirmation, something has gone wrong at our end. Please telephone (207) 555-0131 or email help@easyreachhome.com and we will find out what and tell you plainly.

If your order is going to be late

The Federal Trade Commission's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule sets out exactly what a seller must do when it cannot ship on time. Rather than leave our obligations implied, here they are in full.

  • Any shipping time we publish is one we hold a reasonable basis for meeting. If a page gives no time at all, the order goes out inside 30 days of us receiving it complete.
  • If we are going to miss the date we gave you, we will contact you on or before that date. The notice will give either a definite revised shipping date or a plain statement that we cannot give one, and it will tell you that you may cancel for a full refund at no cost to you.
  • If the revised date is 30 days or less beyond the original date, you do not have to reply. Silence is treated as agreement to the short delay, and you keep the right to cancel at any time before the order ships.
  • If the revised date is more than 30 days beyond the original, or if we cannot give a revised date at all, your order is cancelled and refunded automatically unless you expressly tell us you would rather wait.
  • If we then cannot meet the revised date either, we will write to you again before it passes, and at that second stage silence is not agreement. Without your express consent the order is cancelled and refunded.
  • Cancelling is deliberately easy. Reply to the notice, telephone (207) 555-0131, or email help@easyreachhome.com. We will not ask you for a reason.

When an order is cancelled for delay, you are refunded in full to the original payment method within 7 working days, with nothing deducted.

Damaged, lost and late parcels

### Damaged in transit Email help@easyreachhome.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photograph of both the damaged item and the outer carton. We will send a replacement if we have the item in stock, or refund you in full if we do not, and we pay all the postage either way. You do not need to send the damaged item back unless we specifically ask. Please keep the packaging until we have replied, because the carrier sometimes asks to see it.

Tracking says delivered but nothing arrived

Please check with anyone else at the address and look in the usual alternative places first, because most of these turn up within a day. If nothing has appeared after three business days, contact us. We will open a claim with the carrier ourselves and, whatever the carrier concludes, you will get either a replacement or a refund. Chasing the carrier is our job, not yours.

Tracking has stopped moving

If nothing has updated for seven consecutive business days, tell us and we will open a lost-in-transit claim. Replacement or refund, whichever you prefer.

We sent the wrong item

Entirely our error. A prepaid label and the right item go out together, and nothing about putting it right comes out of your pocket.

Getting the address right

Please check your delivery address before you order. If a parcel comes back to us as undeliverable because the address was wrong or incomplete, we refund the goods in full and deduct only the actual return-transit cost the carrier charged us, which we will show you on the carrier's invoice. Where the mistake is ours, we ship again at our own cost.

We cannot change an address once a parcel has shipped. If you spot a mistake before it goes, telephone us straight away and we can fix it.

Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout from your delivery address, where applicable, and shown as a separate line before you confirm. It is never folded into the product price or the shipping charge. As a Maine business we collect Maine sales and use tax, and we collect in other states where economic-nexus thresholds require it.
