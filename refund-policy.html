Returns and refunds

Thirty days from delivery, change of mind included. What can come back, who pays the postage, and when the money arrives.

The short version

You have 30 days from the day your order is delivered to send anything back, provided it is unused and in its original packaging. Changing your mind is a perfectly good reason and we will not ask you to justify it.

If the fault is ours, we pay the postage both ways and refund everything including your original shipping. If you simply changed your mind, you pay the return postage and we refund the price of the goods. We never charge a restocking fee.

Everything below is the detail. If any of it is unclear, telephone (207) 555-0131 and ask before you send anything back.

When the clock starts

The 30 days run from the delivery date the carrier records, not from the day you ordered and not from the day you unpacked the box. Your return request must reach us inside those 30 days; the parcel itself then has a further 14 days to reach us.

If you are buying something as a present and the 30 days will run out before the occasion, tell us when you order and we will note a longer window on the account. We would much rather do that at the start than argue about it later.

What condition it needs to be in

- Unused. Not cooked with, not stood on, not hung on a wall, not put on a bathroom floor, not slept beside. - Complete. Every piece, the liner, the pouch, the lens cloth, the harp and finial, the instruction card. - In its original packaging, with the inner protection. - Undamaged by you. Unpacking it, looking at it and putting it back is entirely expected. A scratched lens is not.

The outer shipping carton does not need to be in perfect condition — you were meant to open it.

If something reaches us used or incomplete, we will photograph it, email you, and offer either a partial refund that reflects the condition or the item sent back to you at our expense. We will not simply keep it and refuse the refund.

The items we cannot take back

- Bath mats that have been used on a bathroom floor. A mat that has been walked on wet cannot be sold to anybody else as new. Mats still folded as supplied are fully returnable. - The seat cushion once the cover has been removed and washed. Unwashed and still in its packaging, it is fully returnable. - Anything marked final sale. There is nothing on this site marked final sale at the time of writing. If there ever is, the product page will say so in plain words directly above the Add to Cart button.

None of that applies to anything faulty. A cracked lens, a kettle that will not switch off, a clock that loses time, a stool with a loose joint, a mat with a backing that was already crumbling — all returnable however far into the 30 days you are, and however much they have been used.

How to start a return

1. Email help@easyreachhome.com with Return in the subject line, within 30 days of delivery. Or telephone (207) 555-0131 during Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 6:00pm Eastern Time and we will do the whole thing with you on the call. 2. Tell us four things: your order number (it looks like ER-50942 and is in your confirmation email), which item you are returning, how many, and whether it is faulty or a change of mind. If it is faulty, attach a photograph. 3. We reply within one business day with a Returns Number, the address to send it to, and — where the fault is ours — a prepaid shipping label attached as a PDF. 4. Write the Returns Number on the outside of the parcel. Pack the item in its own packaging inside an outer box and send it with a tracked service, because we cannot refund a parcel that never arrives. 5. Email us the tracking number and we will confirm when it reaches us.

Please do not send anything back without a Returns Number. It is not bureaucracy for its own sake — unlabelled parcels arrive at a unit that also takes in stock deliveries and they genuinely get lost.

Who pays the return postage

We pay, and we refund your original shipping too :: The item arrived damaged. It is faulty or fails in normal use inside the window. We sent you the wrong thing. The item is materially different from the description on this site. You pay, and we refund the price of the goods :: You changed your mind. The handle turned out to be thicker than you wanted, and the measurement was correctly stated. The clock is too large for the wall. You ordered two and needed one.

A change-of-mind return usually costs $7 to $12 for small items and $20 to $38 for the step stool, the kettle, the lamp or the baskets, paid by you directly to the carrier. We take no cut of that and we do not mark it up.

If your order originally qualified for free shipping over $39 and a change-of-mind return drops the part you keep below $39, we will not go back and charge you the $5.50. Clawing that back afterwards is a sharp practice and we do not use it.

How and when the money comes back

Refunds go to the original payment method, because that is what keeps your card's chargeback protection intact. We cannot refund to a different card or send a cheque.

Order cancelled before it shipped
Refunded in full within 7 working days of the cancellation, including any shipping charge.
Returned item, our fault
Refunded in full including your original shipping, within 5 business days of the parcel arriving with us.
Returned item, change of mind
Price of the goods refunded within 5 business days of arrival. The original shipping charge is not refunded.
Damaged on arrival, refunded rather than replaced
Refunded in full including shipping, within 3 business days of us seeing your photographs. You do not have to wait for the item to travel back.

After we release a refund, your bank or card issuer decides how quickly it shows up — usually three to five business days, occasionally a full statement cycle. That part is outside our control, but we will give you the refund reference to pass on if your bank asks for it.

Exchanges

We do not run a formal exchange process. With sixteen products it is slower than the obvious alternative: place a new order for the item you want and return the first one under this policy. That way the replacement is already on its way while the original travels back, rather than the two happening one after the other.

If the exchange is needed because we sent you the wrong thing, none of that applies. The correct item goes out straight away, you place nothing and you pay nothing.

Faults, and what is not a fault

Report transit damage within 14 days of delivery with photographs of the item and the carton. For something that fails in ordinary use, tell us as soon as it happens and within the 30-day window. We will ask what happened and look at photographs, and where it is a manufacturing fault we replace or refund at our cost.

These are not faults, and it is fairer to name them here than to argue about them later. Every one of them is written as a care instruction on the relevant product page.

  • Beech utensil handles split after dishwasher use. They are raw wood and the page says hand wash only.
  • A carbon steel peeler blade spotted with rust after being left wet. The page says dry it immediately.
  • A bath mat backing cracked and shedding after hot tumble drying. The page says low heat or line dry.
  • A Fresnel magnifier sheet creased or scratched. The page says store it flat in its sleeve and never fold it.
  • A glass magnifier lens hazed by household glass cleaner. The page says use the supplied cloth only.
  • A kettle furred with limescale. That is your water, not the kettle. The page gives the descaling method.
  • A step stool joint loosened after outdoor use or use on an uneven floor. The page says flat, dry, level floors indoors only.
  • Seagrass baskets that have mildewed in a damp room. The page says keep them out of damp.

Cancelling an order

Before your order ships, telephone (207) 555-0131 or email help@easyreachhome.com with your order number and we will cancel it and refund you in full within 7 working days. We will not ask why.

Once you have a tracking number the parcel has gone and it becomes a return instead. You can refuse delivery if you prefer — it comes back to us and we refund the goods — but refused parcels take two to three weeks to find their way home, so an email to us is much faster.

If we cannot resolve it between us

Email help@easyreachhome.com with Escalation in the subject line and your order number. An owner will read it personally and reply within two business days. There is no department to be transferred to; there are three of us.

Nothing on this page limits any right you have under Maine consumer law or the consumer protection law of your own state.
