About Easy Reach Home

Three people in Portland, Maine, sixteen products, and one firm rule about what we will never say.

Who we are

Easy Reach Household Supply LLC trades as Easy Reach Home from a unit on Beacon Pond Road in Portland, Maine. Three of us work here. We pick and pack the orders and we answer our own telephone.

We are a retailer. Everything here is bought in from manufacturers and importers. We do not make anything and we will not imply a workshop we do not have.

How we choose what to stock

Sixteen products. Something earns a place by passing three tests, and the first one is the one that matters most.

  1. Can we state the measurement that makes it worth buying? Not "easy-grip" but 1.3 inches across. Not "large print" but 1.8-inch numerals. Not "powerful magnifier" but a 3.5-inch ground glass lens at 3x with a 3.5-inch working distance. If the supplier cannot give us that number, we assume there is nothing behind the adjective and we pass.
  2. Is the trade-off stated? Glass lenses are clearer and heavier than acrylic. Carbon steel cuts better and rusts if left wet. A flat-woven bath mat washes hot but has no backing at all. Both halves go on the page.
  3. Can it be described without a single claim about the customer? If the only way to sell something is to tell somebody what they can no longer do, it does not go on this shelf.

The rule this whole site is built on

Everything we sell is a convenience and comfort product. Nothing here is a medical device, a mobility aid, an assistive device or a safety device, and nothing here is sold as one.

That is not a disclaimer at the bottom of a page. It changes how every product on the site is written. Here is the difference in practice:

We will never write
prevents falls; keeps you safe; reduces pain; relieves arthritis; improves posture; corrects posture; helps failing eyesight; improves vision; doctor recommended; therapist approved; helps with memory; mobility aid; assistive device; medical grade; senior safety
We write instead
sprayed natural latex backing in a dotted pattern; 1.3-inch turned beech handle; 12-degree wedge; 2.5-inch foam core; 3x magnification over a 3.5-inch glass lens; 1.8-inch numerals, black on matt white; 300 lb rated load; 32-inch reach

The reason is partly legal — health claims about non-medical products breach the Google Ads healthcare policy and are a Federal Trade Commission problem besides — and partly that those claims are simply not true of a spoon or a mat, and we are not going to pretend otherwise to make a sale.

The same applies to safety. Our bath mat has a natural latex backing, which is a fact about the material. What it does on your floor, when your floor is wet, with whatever is on your feet, is not something a shop can honestly promise. So we describe the backing and stop there.

And a rule about how we talk

You will not find the words "senior", "elderly", "the aged", "as we age", or "for those who struggle" anywhere on this site. Not in the product copy, not in the category names, not in the advertising.

Our products suit people who would rather have a thicker handle and a bigger numeral. That is a preference, and preferences do not need a diagnosis attached. A 34-year-old cook with cold hands in January wants the same spoon. Writing as though our customers are a problem to be managed would be both patronising and, as it happens, bad business.

Making the text bigger

Every page has A and A+ buttons at the top. A+ increases the type size across the whole site by a quarter, and the choice is remembered in your browser as you move from page to page. It is in the header rather than buried in a settings menu, because a text-size control nobody can find is not a text-size control.

Your browser's own zoom works too, of course, and this site is built to stay usable at 200 per cent.

Where orders ship from

Every parcel is packed and posted from Portland, Maine. We ship within the United States only — all fifty states, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico — and we ship to PO boxes and to APO, FPO and DPO addresses, with the size exceptions listed on the shipping policy page.

Most orders leave within one business day. The kettle, the lamp, the laundry basket and the storage baskets take one to two, and the step stool takes two because it ships assembled in an oversize carton.

Other things we will not claim

- No origin claims. We do not print "Made in USA" on anything, because we do not hold documentation meeting the FTC's "all or virtually all" standard. We can tell you where a parcel ships from, which is a different statement. - No HSA or FSA eligibility statements, because these are not medical products and are not eligible. - No Medicare, insurance or reimbursement mentions of any kind. - No reviews, star ratings or testimonials, because we have no genuine ones yet. - No urgency. No countdown timers, no stock counters, no crossed-out prices we never charged.

Accessibility commitment

This site is built to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.1 at Level AA. It is the highest-contrast of the sites we have built and it is tested, not assumed.

Body text starts at 20 pixels — larger than our other sites — with a line height of 1.7, and the A+ control takes it to 25. Text contrast is far above the minimum: near-black on white is roughly 17 to 1, navy on white roughly 13.7 to 1, and the muted grey we use for secondary text still better than 9 to 1. There is no light-grey small print anywhere. Yellow is never used as text on a light background; where a yellow button carries a label, the label is near-black at roughly 9.6 to 1.

Every interactive target is at least 48 pixels tall and most are 56 or 60. Navigation is a row of bordered rectangular buttons rather than plain links, because a large target with a visible edge is easier to hit. The focus indicator is deliberately loud: a navy outline inside a thick yellow halo, visible against both the white pages and the navy blocks. Every control is a real button or link and the whole site works from the keyboard. A skip-to-content link is first in the tab order on every page. Every image has meaningful alternative text and explicit width and height. Colour never carries meaning on its own. The layout reflows to 320 pixels with no horizontal scrolling and stays usable at 200 per cent zoom.

Nothing moves on its own: no carousels, no pop-ups on load, no infinite scroll, no animation you cannot escape.
