About this one

A wooden step stool for the kitchen or the closet. Solid beech throughout — not veneered ply — with mortise-and-tenon joints at every corner and a screwed-and-glued stretcher across the back.

The top tread is 17.5 inches from the floor and measures 15 by 9 inches, which is a full foot's width plus room to turn. The lower tread is at 8.5 inches and is 15 by 7. Both treads are machined with shallow grooves running front to back, and the tread edges are rounded over.

There are rubber feet on all four legs. Rated load is 300 pounds, tested to a static load well above that; the rating is on a label under the top tread.

This is a **step stool**, not a stepladder and not a chair. It has no handrail. Use it on a flat, dry, level floor, face the steps when you come down, and do not stand on the top tread to reach sideways. Those are the manufacturer's use instructions and they are printed on the card in the box.

Everything we sell is a household convenience product. It is not a medical device, a mobility aid or a safety device, and we make no claim that it prevents, treats or improves anything. What is on this page is the material, the measurements and what is in the box.
