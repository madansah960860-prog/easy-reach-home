About this one

A Fresnel sheet is a flat plastic lens with the curvature cut into concentric rings, so it magnifies like a thick convex lens while being 0.5 millimetres thick. This one is 8.3 by 10.6 inches — a whole letter page — at 2× magnification.

It works differently from a hand magnifier and it is worth knowing which you want. Lay it flat on the page and it enlarges the whole page at once at a lower power. Lift it two or three inches and the magnification increases but the field narrows. It will never be as optically clean as a ground glass lens; what it gives you instead is a whole page at once and something that weighs under an ounce.

It is flexible enough to roll loosely and it slips into a book to double as a bookmark. Being plastic it scratches, so it comes in a card sleeve and should go back in it.

There is a ruled 1-inch scale printed along one long edge, which is useful for measuring small things on the page.
