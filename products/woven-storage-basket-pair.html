About this one

Two baskets, sized to fit a standard 14-inch shelf with a little room to slide them in and out. Each is 13 inches wide, 10 deep and 9 tall, woven from seagrass over a steel wire frame so it keeps its shape when empty.

Both have a cut-out handle in the front face, 3.5 inches wide, which is the detail that makes a shelf basket usable — you pull it out towards you with one hand rather than reaching over the top of a full basket to lift it.

They are the plain useful thing for the top of a wardrobe, a hall shelf, the bottom of a linen cupboard, or under a side table. Seagrass has a natural variation in colour and a faint hay smell when new, which fades within a week or two.

No liner, no lid. They are designed to be pulled out and looked into.

    • Dust with a dry brush. Wipe with a barely damp cloth only if you must, and dry at once.
