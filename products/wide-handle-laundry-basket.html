About this one

The handles are the reason to buy this rather than a plastic hamper. They are cut through the weave on both sides, 4 inches wide and 1.8 inches deep, and bound with a leather-look trim so they do not fray. You get a whole hand in, which matters far more than it sounds when the basket is full and you are going down a hallway.

It holds 60 litres, which is roughly two full machine loads, in a woven bamboo body over a steel frame. The frame is the reason it holds its shape empty and does not collapse into a heap the way an unframed fabric hamper does.

The liner is unbleached cotton, attached with four ties, and it comes out to go in the wash itself. Bamboo catches on knitted fabric, so the liner is doing real work rather than being decorative.

It is 22 inches tall and weighs 4.1 pounds empty. There is no lid. We would rather sell a basket that is easy to drop something into than one with a lid you have to lift first.

  • How to look after it

    • Machine wash the liner cool and line dry it. Do not wash the bamboo.
    • Dust the bamboo with a dry brush; wipe with a barely damp cloth only if you must, and dry it.
    • Keep it out of a wet shower zone. Standing water will darken and eventually split bamboo.
