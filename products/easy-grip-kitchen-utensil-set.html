About this one

The handle is the entire reason this set exists. A standard kitchen spoon has a handle about 0.6 inches across; these are turned beech at 1.3 inches, with a slight barrel shape so your hand closes around something rather than pinching it.

That is a measurement, not a claim. Whether a thicker handle suits you is your call, and the useful thing we can do is give you the number so you can compare it against a spoon already in your drawer.

Five pieces: a slotted turner, a solid spoon, a slotted spoon, a ladle and a pasta server. The heads are 18/8 stainless steel, riveted through the handle with three brass rivets, so the join does not work loose the way a glued ferrule does. Each handle has a 0.5-inch hanging hole drilled through the end.

Beech is untreated, so it wants hand washing and an occasional wipe of food-safe mineral oil. Dishwasher heat will dry raw wood out and eventually split it, which is why we say hand wash rather than putting a dishwasher-safe symbol on something that technically survives twenty cycles and then does not.

Everything we sell is a household convenience product. It is not a medical device, a mobility aid or a safety device, and we make no claim that it prevents, treats or improves anything. What is on this page is the material, the measurements and what is in the box.
