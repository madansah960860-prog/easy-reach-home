About this one

A plain seat cushion for a hard chair. It is 16 inches square and 2.5 inches thick, filled with a 30-density polyurethane foam core wrapped in a polyester fibre layer — the foam gives it structure so it does not flatten out in a month, and the fibre wrap stops the foam edge feeling like a step.

There are ties at two corners, 14 inches long, so it can be tied to a chair back or to the rear legs and stays where it is put. Ties are far more reliable on a wooden or metal chair than the non-slip patches some cushions use.

The cover is a woven cotton-polyester blend with a concealed zip along the back edge, so the whole cover comes off for washing while the foam stays out of the machine. Machine washable cool.

It is a comfort cushion and that is all it is. We are not going to describe it as orthopaedic, posture-correcting, pressure-relieving or therapeutic, because it is none of those things and none of those claims would be lawful for us to make.

Everything we sell is a household convenience product. It is not a medical device, a mobility aid or a safety device, and we make no claim that it prevents, treats or improves anything. What is on this page is the material, the measurements and what is in the box.
