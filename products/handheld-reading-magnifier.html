About this one

A plain hand magnifier for small print — a medicine carton, a phone bill, the back of a seed packet, a map. The lens is 3.5 inches across at 3× magnification.

It is **optical glass, not acrylic**, which is the specification that matters. Glass stays clear where acrylic hazes with cleaning, and it produces much less colour fringing at the edge of the field. The trade is weight: this is 6.8 ounces against about 2 for a plastic one.

The lens is a double-convex ground lens, not a moulded Fresnel sheet, so the whole field is in focus rather than only the middle. Working distance at 3× is roughly 3.5 inches from the page — that is where to hold it for a sharp image.

The frame is aluminium with a turned hardwood handle 1.2 inches across and 4.5 inches long. There is no light in it, which keeps the weight and the price down and means there is no battery to go flat.

This is a household magnifier for reading small print. It is not an optical or medical device, and choosing eyewear is a matter for an eye care professional, not a shop.
