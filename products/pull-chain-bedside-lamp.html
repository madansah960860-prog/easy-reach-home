About this one

The switch is the reason for this lamp. It is a 10-inch beaded metal chain hanging from the socket, which means you reach for a general area rather than for a small button, and you do not have to find a rocker switch somewhere along a cord that has slid behind the table.

The base is a weighted ceramic drum, 5.5 inches across, and it weighs 3.4 pounds. That is deliberate: a light lamp goes over when somebody pulls the chain, which defeats the whole idea.

The shade is a 9-inch drum in an off-white linen-look fabric with a white lining, which throws light both up and down rather than only down. Overall height is 16 inches.

It takes a standard E26 screw bulb up to 60 watts, or the LED equivalent, and the bulb is **not included** — everybody has a preference on colour temperature and brightness, and a bulb we chose would probably be the wrong one. For a bedside lamp, a 2700K warm white at around 800 lumens is the usual starting point.

The cord is 6 feet with a moulded plug and there is no inline switch on it.

  • What it is made of

    Weighted ceramic base, steel socket with a beaded chain pull switch, linen-look fabric drum shade with a white lining, 6 ft cord.

  • Size and weight

    16 in overall height. Shade 9 in diameter × 7 in tall. Base 5.5 in diameter. Chain 10 in. Weight 3.4 lb. Takes one E26 bulb up to 60 W (not included). 6 ft cord.

  • How to look after it

    • Unplug before changing the bulb or cleaning.
    • Dust the shade with a soft brush. Do not wash the fabric shade.
    • Wipe the ceramic base with a barely damp cloth.
    • Do not exceed the 60 W maximum. An over-wattage bulb will scorch the shade.
    • Keep the cord out of a walkway and do not run it under a rug.
