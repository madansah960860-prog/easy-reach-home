About this one

The opposite approach to our rubber-backed mat, and a deliberate alternative. This is a flat-woven cotton rug — reversible, no backing, no latex — which means it washes hot, dries fast, and has nothing on it that can perish or crack over time.

It is 24 by 36 inches, larger than the rubber-backed mat, in a chunky flat weave at 1,000gsm with a 0.3-inch profile. Flat-woven means it does not hold water in a pile, so it goes from soaked to dry considerably faster than a tufted mat.

Because it has **no non-slip backing**, it is meant for a floor where that is not a concern, or for use with a separate rug underlay, which is sold in any hardware shop. We are saying that plainly rather than leaving you to discover it: if you specifically want a backing, buy the rubber-backed mat instead.

It is reversible, so it is effectively two mats, and the ends are hand-knotted rather than hemmed.

Everything we sell is a household convenience product. It is not a medical device, a mobility aid or a safety device, and we make no claim that it prevents, treats or improves anything. What is on this page is the material, the measurements and what is in the box.
