About this one

This is the same idea as our 12-inch clock in reverse: **white numerals on a black face**. Some people find light-on-dark noticeably easier to pick out, particularly in a bright room or against a pale wall, and it is worth knowing that the choice exists rather than assuming black-on-white is always the answer.

It is 14 inches across with 2.2-inch numerals, which is the largest face we stock. The minute track is a mid grey so it reads as secondary. The hands are white with a fine black outline and there is a yellow sweep second hand, which stands out against black better than a red one does.

Matt black dial, non-reflective acrylic lens, aluminium case with a brushed finish. Silent sweep quartz movement — no tick — on one AA battery, not included, for around eighteen months. Single keyhole on the back.

One honest caveat: a black-faced clock disappears against a dark wall. If your wall is dark, the 12-inch white-faced clock is the better buy, and we would rather say so than sell you the more expensive one.
