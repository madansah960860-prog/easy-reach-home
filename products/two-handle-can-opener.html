About this one

A side-cut opener runs the cutting wheel around the outside of the rim rather than through the lid. Two things follow from that: the lid comes off with no sharp edge on it at all, and the opener never touches the food.

The handles are 1.2 inches across with a cushioned covering, and the turning knob is 1.4 inches in diameter with deep knurling, which is noticeably larger than the little butterfly wing most openers use. Again, those are measurements rather than claims — compare them against whatever is in your drawer.

The gears are stainless and the cutting wheel is hardened steel. It grips the rim when you squeeze the handles together and releases when you let go, and it lifts the lid off with a small magnet on the underside so you are not fishing for it.

It handles cans from about 2 to 6 inches in diameter, which covers everything from a tomato paste tin to a large coffee can. It is 8 inches long and weighs 8.4 ounces.
