What this policy covers

This explains how Easy Reach Household Supply LLC, trading as Easy Reach Home, handles personal information collected through easyreachhome.com, through our order emails, through our occasional mailing list, and over the telephone on (207) 555-0131.

We are a three-person household goods shop. We are not an advertising business, and this website loads no analytics tag, no advertising pixel, no social media embed and no third-party script of any kind. That is a verifiable property of the pages — you can confirm it by viewing the source — rather than a promise about our intentions.

The information we hold

### Information you give us Order information :: Your name, delivery address, billing address, email address and telephone number. We need these to pack a parcel, address it and tell you when it left. Payment information :: Card details are entered on our payment processor's own hosted page. Your full card number never reaches our servers and is never stored by us. We receive only the last four digits, the card brand, whether the authorisation succeeded and the amount, which is what we need to match a payment to an order and to issue a refund. Contact form messages :: Your name, your email address, the order number if you choose to give one, and whatever you wrote. Mailing list :: Your email address and the date you asked to be added. Telephone orders :: The same order information, written down by hand and then entered into the order system.

Information collected automatically

Our web host keeps standard server logs: the requesting IP address, the date and time, the file requested, the HTTP status code, the referring page and the browser's user-agent string. They exist for security and for diagnosing faults, are kept for 30 days, and are then deleted.

This site sets no cookies at all, first-party or third-party. Two things are stored in your own browser and never transmitted to us:

  • Your cart, held in `localStorage`. It stays on your device and we cannot see it.
  • Your text size choice, held in `localStorage` under the key `erh_textsize`, so that pressing A+ once keeps the larger type as you move between pages. It records nothing except the word "large" or nothing at all.

Clearing your browser data removes both.

What we use it for

- Packing, addressing and sending your order, and handling any return or refund. - Sending transactional email: an order confirmation, a shipping confirmation with tracking, a delay notice if one becomes necessary, and refund confirmations. - Answering the question you sent us, or telephoning you back. - Sending the occasional mailing, if and only if you asked for it, and until you ask us to stop. - Keeping the records that tax and accounting law require us to keep. - Detecting and preventing fraudulent orders.

We do not build advertising profiles, we do not profile customers in any sense, and we make no automated decisions about anybody.

Cookies, and why there is no banner

There is no cookie banner on this site because there are no cookies to consent to. Nothing here tracks you.

The two `localStorage` items described above are browser storage features rather than cookies: they are not sent with any request, they are not readable by anybody else, and they exist purely so that your cart and your text size survive a page change.

A Global Privacy Control signal or a Do Not Track header is respected here by default. The plain reason is that this site runs no cross-context tracking for either of them to turn off.

Who else sees your information

Only the small number of service providers it takes to actually send a parcel. Each is engaged as a service provider or processor, and each may use the information only to perform that service, never for its own purposes.

Payment processor
Receives your name, billing address, email address and card details directly from you on its own hosted page. Needed to take payment and issue refunds.
Shipping carriers, USPS and UPS
Receive your name, delivery address, telephone number and the parcel weight. Needed to deliver the parcel and provide tracking.
Email delivery provider
Receives your email address and the content of transactional emails and of the mailing list.
Web host
Holds the site files and the 30-day server logs described above.
Accountant and tax authorities
Receive order and payment records, as the law requires.

We will also disclose information where we are legally required to — a valid subpoena, court order or lawful request — and where we are permitted to tell you, we will. If the business were ever sold, customer records could transfer to the buyer as part of it, and we would email everybody affected before that happened.

Selling personal information is something this business has never done, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising either. That holds for every customer, at every age, including everyone under 16.

Your rights, honoured for every customer

A number of states now give their residents privacy rights. Rather than checking where you live first, we honour all of the following for every customer.

  • The right to know — what categories and specific pieces of personal information we hold about you, where they came from, why we have them, and who we have disclosed them to.
  • The right to delete — subject only to the records that tax, accounting and fraud-prevention law require us to keep.
  • The right to correct — anything that is inaccurate.
  • The right to opt out of sale or sharing — there is nothing to opt out of, because we do neither. That is why you will find no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link on this site: the practice it would switch off does not occur here.
  • The right to limit the use of sensitive personal information — we collect none as the statutes define it. No Social Security number, no financial account number, no precise geolocation, and no health data of any kind. We are a household goods shop; we have no reason to ask you anything about your health and we never do.
  • The right not to be discriminated against — exercising any of these rights will never change your price, your service or whether we accept your order.
  • The right to appeal — if we refuse a request we will tell you why and how to appeal, and an owner will review the appeal personally.

These rights are available to California residents under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, and on substantially similar terms to residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with comprehensive privacy legislation.

How to make a request

Email help@easyreachhome.com with Privacy request in the subject line, or telephone (207) 555-0131 during Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 6:00pm Eastern Time. Tell us which right you are exercising and give us the email address or order number you used, so that we can find your records.

We will acknowledge your request within 10 days and respond substantively within 45 days. If we genuinely need longer, we will tell you inside those 45 days and take up to a further 45 days, for a maximum of 90 days in total. If you are asking on somebody else's behalf as an authorised agent, we will ask for written proof of your authority and will verify directly with the person concerned.

We do not charge for a privacy request. If one were manifestly unfounded or excessive we could charge a reasonable fee, but we would explain and agree it with you first rather than simply invoicing.

How long we keep information

Order records :: 7 years, because tax and accounting rules require it. Payment records (last four digits only) :: 7 years, alongside the order. Contact form messages and email :: 24 months from the last message, then deleted. Mailing list address :: Until you unsubscribe, then removed within 10 days. Server logs :: 30 days. Return and refund records :: 7 years, alongside the order.

Security

Our website is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your browser and the site is encrypted. Payment details are entered on the processor's own PCI-DSS compliant hosted page and never reach our systems. Access to order records is limited to the three of us, each with an individual account and multi-factor authentication. The few paper records we keep are locked.

No system is perfectly secure and we are not going to claim otherwise. If a breach affected your personal information, we would notify you and the relevant state authorities as state breach-notification law requires, without undue delay.

Children's privacy

This is a general-audience household goods shop. It is not directed to children under 13, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and nothing on this site is designed to appeal particularly to children.

Some of what we sell is unsuitable for children — a carbon steel peeler blade, a can opener cutting wheel and a glass magnifier that focuses sunlight — and the relevant product pages say so plainly. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has given us information, email help@easyreachhome.com and we will remove it and write back to confirm that we have.

You have to be 18 or over to order from us. That condition is set out in the terms of service.

Changes to this policy

The in-force date at the top of this page always shows the current version. If we make a change that materially affects your rights we will email everyone on our mailing list and place a notice on the home page for 30 days, and we will not apply a materially worse policy retroactively to information already collected.
